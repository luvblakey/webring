What PSI should I run?

The number on the sidewall is a maximum, not a target. Set your weight and tire width for a good starting pressure, then adjust by feel.

A starting point for paved roads. Drop 3–5 psi for gravel or wet weather. Not sure? Stop by and we'll check it on the shop gauge for free.

How a repair works

No surprises on the bill

  1. Drop off

    Book a slot online or just roll in. We tag your bike and note what you're hearing or feeling.

  2. We inspect

    Within a day we text a quote with photos of anything worn out, ranked by what matters most.

  3. You approve

    Reply yes, no, or "just the brakes." We only do the work you sign off on.

  4. Pick up

    We test ride it, text you when it's ready, and hold it free for a week.
